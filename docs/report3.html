Byproduct experiment, version 3: weights evolve. Checks only.

All three surviving theories say intelligence was a byproduct. A faithful simulation therefore scores agents only on staying alive and being copied, then measures general ability separately on held-out tests.

With the 1.5B model, selection on survival alone moved near-task energy by +8.4 against chance parents (SOLO 29.7, DRIFT 21.3, unmodified 21.9; SOLO ahead in 4 of 5 seeds). General ability did not follow: SOLO 0.351, DRIFT 0.356, unmodified 0.325.

The ceiling check failed for both models on the 3-family world: even training directly on correct answers did not lift the held-out tests. On a world widened to 16 families the result is partial: families never trained on rose by +0.054 (SE 0.016) over a control trained on the 3-family world, while the three original tests show no effect of breadth (+0.000, SE 0.018). Direct training is the upper bound for selection, so the full 7-arm run is still not justified at this model size.

What changed from the written design

  • Compute moved from the Mac to Modal: first CPU workers with a $10 limit, then one GPU worker once you approved it.
  • The 0.5B CPU runs used an easier world (plants need 1 step). The 1.5B GPU runs use the designed world.
  • Replies are parsed leniently: a bare answer without JSON counts.
  • After the first ceiling check failed, the world was widened to 16 families and the ceiling check was repeated with a control arm.
  • Only DRIFT and SOLO were built. The five social arms were not, because the ceiling check failed first.
  • Retention is a hand-made set of sums, capitals and comparisons, not a public benchmark.

Limits

  • Models of 0.5B and 1.5B parameters cannot stand in for a frontier model. A null result here does not transfer.
  • Two of the three held-out tests sit at chance for both models, so general ability rests mostly on code-breaking.
  • One adapter per population, so heredity is at population level.
  • The wider-world check is one training run per arm. Its standard errors cover test items only.
  • The 6 held-out families were written by the same author in the same style as the 13 trained ones, so they measure transfer to nearby tasks.
  • Costs are my own upper estimate. Modal's bill is the authority.
